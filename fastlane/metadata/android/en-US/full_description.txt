Skylight Wallet is an open-source and self-custody wallet for Monero created by MAGIC Grants, a public charity.

FEATURES
* A beautiful, modern interface available in light and dark themes.
* Fully self-custody. Only you have your private keys and seed, not us.
* Completely open-source, with reproducible builds.
* No local wallet scanning needed! Syncing can be handled by the LWS you specify. Save time, data, and your battery. Alternatively, you can choose to connect to a Monero node and sync locally.
* Built-in Tor support. Optionally connect to your LWS or node over Tor to protect your privacy.
* Address book for managing contacts.
* Background notifications for incoming transactions, and background syncing if you use a remote node.
* OpenAlias, for easy and secure sending without cumbersome addresses.
* Subaddress support.

Skylight Wallet is developed by MAGIC Grants, a 501(c)(3) public charity.
