Skylight Wallet is an open-source and self-custody wallet for Monero created by MAGIC Grants, a public charity.

FEATURES
* A beautiful, modern interface available in light and dark themes.
* Fully self-custody. Only you have your private keys and recovery phrase, not us.
* Completely open-source, with reproducible builds.
* Address book for managing contacts.
* No local wallet scanning needed. You can offload scanning to servers of your choice, or you can scan locally with a node connection if you prefer.
* Built-in Tor support. Optionally connect to services over Tor to protect your privacy.
* Protect your wallet with hardware security keys. When enabled, your spend key and recovery phrase have multiple layers of protection.
* Encrypted backups of your payment details and address book. They are securely encrypted with keys derived from your recovery phrase, so you don't need another backup password.
* Background notifications for incoming transactions and background syncing.
* OpenAlias and payment links, for easy and secure sending without cumbersome addresses.

Skylight Wallet is developed by MAGIC Grants, a 501(c)(3) public charity.
